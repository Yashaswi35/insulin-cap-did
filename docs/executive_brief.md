# Executive Brief: Medicare $35 Insulin Cap, First-Year Impact

**Question:** Did the January 2023 cap on Part D insulin cost sharing lower what members pay, and did it change how much insulin they fill?

**Data:** CMS Part D claims, 2019 to 2023, aggregated by state and drug. This covers 51 jurisdictions, 44 diabetes drugs and 427M claims.

## Findings

1. **Members paid much less.** Average out-of-pocket cost per 30-day insulin fill fell from $19.43 to $14.28 (−26%) in the cap's first year. Cost for other diabetes drugs rose 12% over the same year.
2. **Use rose modestly.** Insulin fills had been declining about 4% a year as patients moved to newer drugs. In 2023 that decline stopped. Fills came in about 4% above trend, and every model variant points the same way, at +4% to +8%.
3. **Results that fail their checks were not used.** A standard before-and-after comparison against other diabetes drugs showed a 7% *drop*. It failed its own validity tests, because insulin was already losing share before the cap. Reporting it would have reversed the conclusion.

## Confidence

| Finding | Confidence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lower out-of-pocket cost | High | The drop is large, immediate, and lines up with the policy dates |
| About 4% higher use | Moderate | It passes a timing placebo. A drug class placebo suggests ±3 points of error in the trend model. |
| Larger effect where the cap bites hardest | Not shown | The age comparison showed no break. State-level data can't isolate members without the low-income subsidy. |

## Implications for plan design

- **Copay caps reliably reduce member cost burden quickly.** That should be the primary value case.
- **Expect a modest volume response.** Don't size the business case on fill growth.
- **Evaluate the next program on member-level claims.** Use adherence (proportion of days covered), discontinuation and downstream outcomes. Choose a comparison group whose pre-period trend matches the treated group, and confirm that with placebo tests before reporting an effect.

## Next steps

- Add the 2024 CMS file, when posted, to test whether the break persists.
- Repeat the analysis on member-level data to measure adherence and to separate members with and without the low-income subsidy.
